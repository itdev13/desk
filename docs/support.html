★ What HelmDesk does

HelmDesk is a helpdesk and ticketing layer that sits on top of your conversations. When a customer messages one of your connected channels, HelmDesk automatically opens a ticket — a trackable record with a status, priority, owner, and response deadline — so nothing gets lost in a busy inbox.

Every message becomes a ticket

Inbound messages across your support channels are captured automatically and turned into tickets.

One place to work

Reply, add internal notes, set priority, assign an owner, and resolve — all from a single workspace.

Stay on top of SLAs

Each ticket gets a response and resolution deadline. Overdue tickets are flagged automatically.

Fully your brand

On the Agency plan, rename the app, set your color, and give clients a branded intake form.

1 First-time setup

The first time you open HelmDesk, a short setup wizard walks you through four quick steps. HelmDesk won't create any tickets until setup is finished — so take a minute to get this right.

  1. Channels — choose which support channels HelmDesk should watch (Email, SMS, Live Chat, Web Chat, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google). All channels are on by default; turn off anything you don't use for support.
  2. Filters — decide what should not become a ticket. Ignore automated/marketing replies, ignore very short one-word messages, and set keyword lists that force (or skip) ticket creation.
  3. Assignment — pick how new tickets are routed: round-robin across your team, to one specific agent, or left unassigned for agents to pick up.
  4. SLA & lifecycle — set response and resolution targets per priority, when resolved tickets auto-close, and your ticket number prefix.

Reinstalling? If you ever remove and re-add HelmDesk within the retention window, the wizard pre-fills everything from your last saved settings — just confirm and go live.

3 Tickets & how they're created

A ticket is a single customer request you're tracking to resolution. Tickets get created three ways:

Automatically, from inbound messages

This is the main path. When a customer messages one of your configured channels, HelmDesk opens a ticket, links it to the customer's contact, and (if enabled) sends an auto-reply. If the same customer already has an open ticket, their new message is added to it instead of creating a duplicate.

From your client portal

If you've turned on the client portal (Agency plan), customers can submit requests through your branded intake form, and each submission becomes a ticket. See section 10.

Manually

Log a ticket yourself from the Queue or Board using New ticket — useful for phone calls or requests that came in off-channel. Enter the subject, contact, channel, priority, and details.

Replies sent outside HelmDesk still count. If a teammate answers a customer from your native inbox or an automation, HelmDesk captures that reply on the ticket and stops the response clock — so your SLA numbers stay accurate.

4 Working in the Inbox

The Inbox is a three-pane workspace and where you'll spend most of your time.

Left — ticket list

Filter by status (All, Open, Waiting, Resolved, Closed) with live counts, and search by subject, contact, or ticket number.

Center — the conversation

The full thread plus a composer. Switch between a Reply (goes to the customer) and an Internal note (team-only).

Right — ticket details

Contact card, priority, assignee, status, any portal form answers, and the SLA panel with a Mark resolved button.

Make it yours

Resize or collapse any pane. The view auto-refreshes so new replies appear without reloading.

Heads up: some ticket types (like phone calls or portal submissions with no reply channel) can't be replied to directly — the composer switches to Internal note only, so your team can still log what happened.

5 Queue & Board

Queue — your work list

A focused, filterable list topped with live KPIs: Open, Overdue, Unassigned, Average first reply, and In-SLA % over 30 days. Use the filter chips to switch between My queue, Unassigned, All open, and Overdue. Agents see only their own tickets.

Board — drag-and-drop Kanban

Tickets laid out in columns — New Open Pending Resolved. Drag a card from one column to another to change its status instantly. Each card shows the ticket number, subject, priority, assignee, SLA state, contact, and last activity.

6 Dashboard & reporting

The Dashboard covers the last 30 days and is the view you can confidently show your own clients.

  • Headline KPIs — open tickets, overdue, resolved in 30 days, average first reply time, and In-SLA %.
  • Created vs. Resolved — a 14-day trend so you can see whether you're keeping up.
  • Open by agent — current workload across the team.
  • Tickets by status and Tickets by channel — where your volume sits.

Agents see metrics for their own tickets only; admins see the whole team.

7 Priorities & SLA targets

How the SLA clock behaves

  • The first-response clock stops as soon as an agent replies (even from outside HelmDesk).
  • The clock pauses while a ticket is Pending (waiting on the customer) or On hold.
  • When a deadline passes, the ticket is flagged Overdue and turns red — a visibility flag so it floats to the top of your queue. Nothing is reassigned automatically.

8 Team & seats

Admins manage the team under the Team tab.

  1. Sync your team — pull your current user roster into HelmDesk with one click.
  2. Mark who's active — toggle each person Active (assignable to tickets) or Excluded. People removed from your CRM show a "Deleted in CRM" badge.
  3. Watch your seats — a bar shows "X of N seats in use." If you try to activate more agents than your plan allows, HelmDesk asks you to switch to a larger plan.

There are two roles: Admins (full access to Settings, Team, and all tickets) and Agents (work only their assigned tickets). Whoever installed HelmDesk is an admin.

9 Settings

Admins configure HelmDesk across three tabs.

Channels & filters

  • Choose which channels count as support channels.
  • Ignore automated/marketing replies and very short one-word messages.
  • Turn the auto-reply on or off and edit its message.
  • Set always-create and never-create keyword lists for fine control over what becomes a ticket.

Assignment & SLA

  • Pick your assignment mode: round-robin, a specific agent, or unassigned.
  • Set first-response and resolution targets per priority, with a plain-English live preview.
  • Set how many days before resolved tickets auto-close, and whether customers can reopen a resolved ticket (Never / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 days).
  • Choose your ticket number prefix (e.g. HD-1042).

Branding & portal (Agency plan)

  • Set your brand name and primary color — they replace HelmDesk's name and accent throughout.
  • Turn on the client portal and build your public intake form.

10 Your client portal (Agency plan)

The client portal is a branded, public web form your customers can use to submit requests — every submission becomes a ticket, automatically linked to the right contact.

Build your form

  1. Go to Settings → Branding & portal and turn the portal on.
  2. Use the form builder to add, reorder, edit, or remove fields. Field types include text, long text, dropdown, radio buttons, and checkboxes.
  3. Map each field to a core detail (name, email, phone, subject, message) or leave it as a custom question — custom answers show up in the ticket's "Form details" panel.
  4. Mark fields required as needed and save. Share your portal link with customers.

The form carries your brand name and color, with a "Powered by {your brand}" footer. HelmDesk protects it from spam automatically.

12 Data & privacy

HelmDesk stores only what it needs to run your helpdesk — a reference to each customer's contact plus the ticket history. It doesn't copy or hold customer data beyond that.

  • If you uninstall, your tickets and settings are kept for a short grace window so a reinstall restores everything.
  • After 7 days, all of your workspace data is permanently and automatically deleted. Reinstalling within the window cancels the deletion.

? Frequently asked questions

Why isn't a message creating a ticket?
Check three things: (1) setup is complete — HelmDesk creates nothing until the wizard is finished; (2) the message arrived on a channel you enabled in Settings → Channels; and (3) it wasn't caught by a filter (automated reply, one-word message, or a "never-create" keyword). You can force specific messages into tickets with an "always-create" keyword.
A customer replied again — will it make a duplicate ticket?
No. If the customer already has an open ticket, their new message is added to it. If their ticket was recently resolved and you allow reopening within the window you set, it reopens that ticket instead of creating a new one.
Can my agents see each other's tickets?
No. Agents only see and work the tickets assigned to them, including on the Dashboard. Admins see everything.
What happens when a ticket goes overdue?
It's flagged as Overdue and highlighted so it rises to the top of your queue. HelmDesk never reassigns or closes it for you based on the deadline — you stay in control.
Can I rebrand HelmDesk as my own?
Yes, on the Agency plan. Set your brand name and primary color under Settings → Branding & portal, and they replace HelmDesk's name and accent across the app and your client portal.
Does auto-reply fire for every ticket?
Auto-reply (when enabled) is sent for tickets created from inbound customer messages. It is not sent for tickets you log manually.
